Case studies

Problem, meet solution.

A closer look at how we approached a few recent projects — the brief, the constraints, and what we delivered.

01Professional services

Andrew J. Smith Realty

Problem

A growing realty office needed a client-facing space that felt as polished as the brand — without losing the room agents needed to actually work.

Solution

A confident reception moment, a mix of private offices and open collaboration, and one consistent material palette tying it all together — specified, supplied and installed by SHIFT.

02Corporate

Baker Tilly, Windsor

Problem

A professional-services firm needed focus space for heads-down work alongside comfortable rooms for client meetings — in an open floorplate prone to noise.

Solution

Teknion systems zoned for privacy, acoustic treatment where it counted, and hospitality-grade meeting areas that make clients feel looked after.

03Walls & PODs

Windsor-Essex Regional Chamber of Commerce

Problem

The Chamber needed enclosed offices and meeting rooms carved out of an open space — but wanted to avoid the cost, downtime and permanence of drywall construction.

Solution

Double-glazed demountable walls delivered daylight, acoustic separation and a premium finish — reconfigurable and reusable if the layout ever needs to shift.

04Non-profit

Regional Non-Profit HQ

Problem

A mission-driven organization needed a warm, welcoming headquarters on a careful budget — flexible enough for staff, volunteers and community events.

Solution

Durable, good-value furniture in an inviting palette, with collaborative zones that reconfigure easily for meetings, training and gatherings.

Case-study narratives are illustrative demo copy — to be confirmed and expanded with SHIFT (scope allows up to five).
